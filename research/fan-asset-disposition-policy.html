PPI Fan Asset Disposition Policy

PPI Risk Desk Oct 8, 2026 2 min read

Following recent market confusion, the PPI Risk Desk has issued formal guidance regarding the disposition of baseballs entering the seating area.

The decision to retain or transfer a caught ball remains at the discretion of the catcher. However, the following framework should assist with responsible asset management:

1. Foul ball hit by the opposing team

Celebrate the catch. If a child wearing the opposing team’s jersey is nearby, transfer the asset. This is considered acceptable interstate diplomacy.

2. Foul ball hit by your team

Keep it or give it to a kid. No material difference. Committee declines jurisdiction.

3. Home run by your team

Keep it. You caught a home run. Congratulations.

Milestone balls should be retained pending valuation. Sentiment is temporary. Arbitrage opportunities are forever.

4. Home run by the opposing team

Have some self-respect. Return the asset to the field immediately. No hostile securities are to be held in the portfolio.

5. A child several rows away wants your ball

No obligation exists. Proximity matters. The Committee does not recognize eminent domain claims against baseballs.

6. You knock a child over, reach across them, or otherwise interfere with their opportunity to catch it

The asset is theirs. Compliance will be monitoring you for the remainder of the game.

7. You catch a ball intended for a specific child by a player

Transfer immediately. This is not a market transaction. You have intercepted a delivery.

8. You already have a ball from that game

Strong presumption toward redistribution. Hoarding provides diminishing marginal utility and terrible optics.

FINAL RULING: Catching a baseball creates ownership. It does not create a mandatory charitable distribution event.

Please govern yourselves accordingly.

— PPI Committee on In-Stadium Asset Allocation & Baseball Ethics
